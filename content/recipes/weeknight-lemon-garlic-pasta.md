servings: 4
summary: "A bright, fast pasta that mostly runs on pantry staples — ready before you'd finish scrolling for takeout."
ingredients:
  - amount: 12
    unit: "oz"
    item: "spaghetti"
  - amount: 4
    unit: "tbsp"
    item: "olive oil"
  - amount: 4
    unit: "cloves"
    item: "garlic, thinly sliced"
  - amount: 1
    unit: ""
    item: "lemon, zested and juiced"
  - amount: 0.5
    unit: "cup"
    item: "grated parmesan"
  - amount: 1
    unit: "pinch"
    item: "red pepper flakes"
  - amount: 1
    unit: "tsp"
    item: "salt, plus more to taste"
instructions:
  - "Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain."
  - "While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes, and cook gently until fragrant, about 2 minutes — don't let the garlic brown."
  - "Add the drained pasta to the skillet along with the lemon zest, lemon juice, and a splash of the reserved pasta water. Toss to coat."
  - "Remove from heat and stir in the parmesan, adding more pasta water as needed until the sauce turns glossy and coats the noodles. Season with salt to taste and serve immediately."
---

Good with a cracked egg on top if you want more of a meal out of it. Leftover lemon halves freeze fine for the next time you need zest.
