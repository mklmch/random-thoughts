For about four minutes every afternoon, the light comes through the stairwell window at exactly the angle that turns the dust into something worth watching. It only lasts as long as it takes to walk up one flight. I've started timing my breaks around it, which feels like a small, slightly absurd victory over a schedule that otherwise owns me.
