This is the first post, which mostly exists to confirm the site actually works end to end: Markdown renders, tags work, and math renders too.

## Why this exists

I read and think about a scattered set of things — philosophy, religion, physics, how people learn — and I wanted somewhere to write down the half-formed version of a thought before I've decided what I think about it. No posting schedule, no theme for the week. Just notes as they come.

## A quick math check

Since physics is one of the things I'll write about here, it's worth confirming inline math like $E = mc^2$ renders correctly, along with display equations:

$$
i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{H} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)
$$

That's the (time-dependent) Schrödinger equation, rendered with [KaTeX](https://katex.org/). To get math rendering on a post, just set `math: true` in the front matter — it's off by default so pages without equations don't load the extra script.
