Built this over about a year of actual late-night drives, so it's less "curated" and more "field-tested." Mostly synth-heavy stuff with a pulse to it — the kind of tracks that make a normal commute feel like it's building to something, even when it's just the grocery store.

Starts slow, picks up around track six, never really comes back down. That's on purpose.
