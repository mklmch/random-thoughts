Every few months I try to "move on" from this album, convinced I've worn it out. It never sticks — I'm back within a couple of weeks. I think it's because it doesn't ask anything of me. I can put it on distracted, annoyed, or half-asleep and it still works.

Track four is doing the most work here, if I'm honest. But you don't get track four without sitting through one through three first, and I've made my peace with that.
